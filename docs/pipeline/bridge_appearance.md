# Bridge appearance

Bridge appearance is a render-only mesh built from an authoritative road deck. It reads the deck edge rows, leaves the deck mesh and colliders unchanged, and skips inferred decks and tunnel floors.

Parapet style comes from the carrying way's source tags. `highway=motorway` and `highway=trunk` infer a continuous concrete parapet; other road classes infer a metal guardrail; foot, cycle, pedestrian, path, and boardwalk ways infer a pedestrian railing with vertical pickets spaced about 0.15 m apart; and a rail-carrying way infers a taller railway parapet. A mapped `barrier=jersey_barrier`, `barrier=guard_rail`, `barrier=railing`, or `barrier=handrail` overrides those defaults. `fence_type=railing` is also accepted. The selected source tag values and whether the style was inferred remain in `BridgeFacts` for provenance. These are procedural fallbacks, not claims about surveyed construction. Long pedestrian bridges can exceed the fixed visual-mesh budget and then return unsupported without a partial mesh.

Bridge structure and parapet style are separate facts. `bridge:structure=truss` describes a load-bearing truss and may add its explicit structural diagonals; an ordinary bridge's safety railing never gets those diagonals. `bridge:material=*` is retained as source information but does not determine parapet construction. OSM documents `bridge:structure` as the load-bearing architecture of spans and distinguishes a road `barrier=guard_rail` from a railing intended to prevent falls. See the [bridge structure key](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Key:bridge:structure), [bridge tagging](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Bridge), and [guard rail tag](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Tag:barrier%3Dguard_rail).

Rails follow the sampled deck edge. Chord intervals are subdivided until the measured three-dimensional deviation of every intervening deck row is at most 0.05 m. This leaves clearance for the outward offset and rail thickness; concrete and railway walls use a wider offset than slimmer rails. The original maximum 80 base panels preserve straight-bridge spacing; curvature can refine them up to a hard limit of 512 panels. Invalid input or an exceeded panel or mesh budget reports the visual as unsupported and returns no partial mesh.

Explicit beam/slab, truss, suspension, and cable-stayed forms remain procedural visual approximations. When full-span sampled deck deviation exceeds 0.05 m, straight truss and cable layouts are suppressed and the visual reports unsupported while retaining only the safe parapets. The generic `bridge:structure=arch` tag does not say whether the arch is above, through, or below the deck. The tag remains in provenance; until a source identifies topology, the renderer emits the selected parapet and reports the structure-specific body as unsupported.

No piers are inferred in lower roads or water, and visual members do not become physical deck or collider geometry.
