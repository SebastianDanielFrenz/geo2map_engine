The key fact today. The game does not read the served carve yet. It fetches raw g2m.terrain.height, smooths it with its own Gaussian and carves roads with its own RoadSurfacePatch. Physics and render both use that game-carved tile. g2m.terrain.carved is ready but has no consumer. Switching the game over is migration phase 7.

2. Terrain steps

T1 Raw DGM1g2m.src.elev.dgm1 at L2: 1024 m tiles of 1 m samples, DHHN2016 heights. This is bare earth, so bridge decks are removed and the gap is interpolated. That leaves a hump under every overpass.g2m:src/layer/src_elev.cpp
T2 Base elevationg2m.elev.base L0: 256 m, 256 x 256, 1 m cells, cell-registered, row 0 south. Int32 in 1/256 m.g2m:src/layer/derive_terrain.cpp:45
T3 Served levelsg2m.terrain.height L0-L6. L0 is elev.base. Each level above is the mean of 4, rounded half to even. Cells are 1, 2, 4 ... 64 m. No roads. This is what the game fetches.g2m:src/layer/derive_terrain.cpp:131
T4 Smoothing (game copy)A Gaussian with radius 2 m, strength 0.35 and 1 pass, at every level. The served carve has its own integer port. Phase 7 deletes the game copy.rg:core/src/terrain_smoothing.cpp, world_terrain.cpp:248
T5 Served carveg2m.terrain.carved L0, per 1 m cell:
1. junction cores (D42) beat approach cores, and the lowest core wins;
2. otherwise the strongest blend, 3-15 m wide and widened to 1:1.5;
3. the ground stays 0.55 m under decks, ramped over 4 m;
4. steep walls at underpass decks (D59).
Bridges and tunnels are never carved, except D65 short cuts (g2m#20).g2m:src/layer/terrain_carve.cpp:743, :860, :1135
T5 Game carveRoadSurfacePatch works on 1 m segments of paved and tunnel profiles. The best road wins by score, RoadSeparation applies at 2.5 m, a 0.35 transition blend runs between roads, crown and verge drop come from the cross-section, and the rg#46 tie-in applies. It runs only up to L2. It does carve tunnels (line 46).rg:core/src/road_surface.cpp:46, :141-199; world_terrain.cpp:229-281
T6 Classg2m.terrain.class L0-L6, 257 x 257 nodes, built from the carve ownership plus land cover. L1-L6 use a rank-max over 3 x 3.g2m:src/layer/terrain_class.cpp:234, terrain_carve.cpp:879
T6 Route stamping (game)Stamps unpaved strips, walkways and standalone paths into the served L0 class tile. A 0.45 m height gate applies, and paved beats walkway beats other.rg:core/src/route_surface_mesh.cpp:376, world_terrain.cpp:841
T7 Physics tileA 255 m tile with 256 x 256 node-registered samples, filled from the resident 256 m tiles. The heightfield is never holed (suppression off).g2m:include/g2m/phys/fill_physics_heights.h:47, bridges/physics_sim/src/g2m_terrain_source.cpp:113, :166
T8 Render chunksLOD chunks from ViewStreamer. They share one surface with Jolt: the 1 m lattice with each cell split on the anti-diagonal. Colours come from the game's table.g2m:src/mesh/terrain_chunk.cpp:210; rg:core/src/terrain_colours.cpp

R1 OSM road graphg2m.roads.graph L2: a 128 m halo, complete ways, midpoint ownership, node tags, restrictions.g2m:src/layer/derive_road_graph.cpp:103
R2 Reference line, lanes, widthsA smooth line per stretch, plus cross-section bands (lanes, shoulders, verges, sidewalks, cycle lanes) from the tags and de.json. g2m#21 bounds the line to its OSM chord (G2M-024).g2m:src/layer/road_reference.cpp:284, road_cross_section.cpp
R3 Vertical profileFitted to DEM samples every 5 m within class grade and curvature limits. A stretch that cannot be fitted is declined. Bridges and tunnels go on to R4.g2m:src/layer/vertical_profile.cpp:30, road_profile.cpp
R4 Completion
Bridges: abutment walk of up to 200 m with a 205 m margin (D48), plus anchors (D47).
TunnelFloor: follows the DEM at 5 m, at most 30 %, radius at least 20 m. From 200 m length it is a straight grade (D52).
Ground ease (D43) and a 20 m retry.
Underpass decks D53-D63: at most 80 m, 2 m abutments.
g2m#20 adds D64 (mask the overpass hump) and D65 (cuts of at most 80 m with a 1-8 m hump).g2m:src/layer/road_completion.cpp:155, :311, :383; road_underpass.cpp:472

R6 Structures and decksDecks for bridges, tunnel floors, underpass decks, inferred roofs (an upper road of rank 5 or more over a tunnel) and approach decks. 5 vertices per 1 m, a 0.5 m slab and fascia sides.g2m:src/world/road_structures.cpp:242, :228
R7 Ribbons and markingsCarriageway, verge, bands, lane paint, arrows, junction patches. Draped on the game-carved ground by bilinear sampling. Decks are required within the physics radius + 255 m and built ahead for a further 600 m, at 16 installs per tick. A deck exists only after the game has derived that L2 road surface, so the start-up gate implicitly waits for road derivation.

5. The physics seam

This is where geo2map data becomes physics_sim objects. Rule G2M-001: geo2map core never depends on physics_sim. Only the opt-in bridge bridges/physics_sim (g2m_ps_bridge) sees ps types.
